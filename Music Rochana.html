Sonic Architecture

Operating under the moniker ChadFat (also known as Rochana Daham ), he is a Sri Lankan music producer and composer recognized for shaping the island’s independent rap, trap, melodic, and contemporary Sinhala soundscapes. Since launching his musical journey in 2021, he has engineered signature records including the high-impact track “Kick Off” with Xayn and Devil Smok, as well as prominent releases such as “Rolling,” “Fugezzi,” “Sandawathiye,” “LSD,” “Thaniwiila,” and “Flash.” Blending surgical audio engineering with distinct urban bounce, his production catalog is defined by dynamic collaborations with prominent beatmakers and artists including Chakra Beatz, Taizy, Clessoff, Akesh Jayasekara, Danlan, Lilalone, Lashan, EDDI3, SANUGA, Radushka, and EskennyWorld20XX.
